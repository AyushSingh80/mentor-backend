# Rubric — Anthropology Optional (Paper 1 and Paper 2)

Score out of 10 for a 150-word answer, out of 15 for a 250-word answer.

The optional carries 500 marks and is marked differently from GS. Do not apply
the GS rubric here.

| Dimension | Weight | What earns marks |
|---|---|---|
| Terminological precision | 25% | Correct disciplinary vocabulary used correctly. Anthropology rewards precise terms; loose paraphrase reads as an undergraduate answer |
| Thinker and school attribution | 20% | Correct attribution — Malinowski's functionalism is not Radcliffe-Brown's structural-functionalism; Lévi-Strauss's structuralism is not either. Misattribution is a serious error, not a slip |
| Paper 1 ↔ Paper 2 linkage | 20% | Theory from Paper 1 anchored to Indian ethnographic material from Paper 2. **This is the single biggest differentiator between a 240 and a 280+ optional score.** Flag every missed opportunity |
| Ethnographic and empirical examples | 20% | Named tribes, named studies, named fieldwork. "Some tribal communities" earns little; "the Jarawa of the Andamans" earns marks |
| Diagrams and presentation | 15% | Kinship charts with correct notation, skull and skeletal morphology, tool typologies, evolutionary trees. Anthropology is a diagram-rewarding paper and most candidates under-use this |

## Paper 1 — theory, biological anthropology, archaeology

Expect: schools of thought and their critiques, evolutionary theory, human
genetics, primatology, prehistoric archaeology, kinship and descent theory,
economic and political organisation, religion and ritual theory, linguistic
anthropology, research methods.

## Paper 2 — Indian anthropology

Expect: Indian ethnography, tribal communities and their classification,
constitutional safeguards, Fifth and Sixth Schedules, PVTGs, tribal development
programmes and their critiques, caste and social structure, applied and
development anthropology, contemporary tribal issues.

## Specific instructions

- When she writes a Paper 1 theory answer with no Indian example, say so.
  Every single time. This habit compounds into large marks.
- When she writes a Paper 2 answer that describes without theoretical framing,
  say so. Description without theory is a Paper 2 answer scoring in the 40s.
- If a kinship question was answered in prose without a chart, treat that as a
  presentation failure and show the chart she should have drawn.
- Her classes are biweekly and she began Anthropology in August 2026. Judge the
  answer, not her stage — but note in your feedback where a gap reflects a
  topic she may not have been taught yet.
